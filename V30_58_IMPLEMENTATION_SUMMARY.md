## Delivered source changes

- Official posted/reversed GL statements in `server/v358-reports.js`: dated trial balance, balance sheet, income statement, comparable prior-period profit, account drill-down with running balance and pagination, management account categories, and a cash-flow activity review. Individual BU scope and company consolidation preserve line-level BU isolation. The company balance sheet eliminates paired inter-BU Due From/To accounts and exposes any unequal net; the cash flow lists internal transfers separately from external operating/investing/financing activities and flags mixed classifications.
- Bank statement CSV/XLSX preview, date/amount/balance validation, duplicate file and line detection, controlled commit and import audit in `server/v319.js`. Import adds bank statement lines only, never Finance or journal rows. The old immediate-upload endpoint returns a migration-to-preview instruction. Existing authorized match/unmatch controls in `server/v290.js` now block closed periods; unmatch requires a reason. `server/v358-control.js` displays separate statement, Finance, GL and configured opening figures, unmatched counts and shared-account/FX flags.
- `server/v358-management-detail.js` adds server-paged machine, import, completed-batch, SKU source, posted expense and posted receivable/payable exposure views and Pink Salt customer invoice and supplier obligation due-date aging linked to actual payments and credit allocations. Customer posted AR follows Finance sale/payment source IDs and direct allocation journals, since sale AR is customer-dimension; unmatched manual customer adjustments remain unattributed. Posted machine profit stays separate from Pakistan resale share; SKU gross margin is explicitly operational and official SKU profit remains unavailable. The general GL exposure view gives net balances and first-posting age. Supplier invoice aging reports source obligation and settlement, while posted AP remains at supplier level rather than falsely assigned to individual imports or packaging receipts.
- `server/v358-source-reconciliation.js` compares current active Pink Salt supplier advances, payroll-run liabilities, Excavator buyer advances and Pink Salt supplier payables with entity/source-linked posted GL; differences and pending journals are explicit. The Accounting Control route links to existing inventory, fixed-asset, financing and inter-BU detail reviews. Buyer receipts minus active allocations and completed refunds, and import/packaging obligations minus actual payments and active supplier-credit allocations, create no cash or balancing journal. The four source comparisons are server-paged at 25/50/100, with BU scope and page-specific exception counts. This remains a current-state comparison, not a historical cutoff ledger.
- Existing monthly close readiness was extended to fixed assets, loans and historical migration checklist areas. CEO reopening requires reason and explicit Review & Confirm and resets signoffs. Year-end review lists all twelve monthly period states and technical blockers for pending journals, unverified money, unmatched statements, open exceptions, unbalanced journals and company inter-BU clearing; accountant signoff still applies and no annual closing journal is posted.
- Historical opening migration through existing Excel/CSV staging, validation and preview. `server/v358-migration.js` previews account and entity matches, FX ties, duplicate source keys and journal balance before proposing; it requires exact active account codes and a balancing debit/credit journal, existing source/entity references when supplied, source-key reservation, BU/date checks and original currency/FX tie-out. CEO proposes a noncash Pending Review journal with source evidence; the existing Posting Control final-post authority applies. A cancelled, never-posted proposal can release reserved keys with audited reason. No automatic equity or suspense offset is invented.
- Dedicated EN/KR Accounting routes under six existing sections for statements, cash flow, management, bank review/import, year-end and historical opening. Existing focused operational, Posting Control and bank matching screens remain reachable.
- Updated 499-row evidence matrix in JSON and XLSX, existing COA collision map and 138-row draft proposal retained unchanged, plus CSV/XLSX bank and opening templates. `qa/staging_v358_existing_data.js` backs up a supplied SQLite file with SQLite’s backup API, copies matching uploads into a temporary directory, boots V30.58 on that isolated copy, reads official reports, and compares retained record hashes, posted GL balances and evidence hashes. It records the known derived supplier-category metadata separately. The source paths are opened read-only and never started as the app database.
