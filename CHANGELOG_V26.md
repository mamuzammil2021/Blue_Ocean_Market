# Blue Ocean Market V26.0.0

## Excavator Buyer Management
- Added a dedicated Buyers tab, visible when the Excavator business unit is selected.
- Buyer profiles with contact, location, country, payment terms and notes.
- Buyer detail view with complete payment history, KRW totals, allocated and available advance balance, linked machines and requirements.
- Buyer payments support KRW, USD and PKR with a payment-time conversion rate and permanent storage of original amount/currency/rate and KRW value.
- Buyer payment receipts are stored and linked to Buyer Documents.
- Buyer requirements can be recorded and removed.
- Sold machines can be linked to a buyer from Sell Machine.
- Added manual Pakistan resale-profit-share records per sold machine: resale price in PKR, manually entered resale profit, share percentage, our share amount, received/outstanding amount, receipt and received marker.
- Resale profit/share is never auto-calculated.

## Existing Excavator flow retained
- Purchase completion remains required before selling.
- Sell Machine remains 100% advance and does not wait for a sale-payment condition.
- Existing sale PDF and Documents behavior retained.
