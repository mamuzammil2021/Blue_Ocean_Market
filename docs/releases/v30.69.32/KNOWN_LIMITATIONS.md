# Operational acceptance and limits

Engineering checks use isolated local servers/databases and synthetic evidence. Live Render, real banks and the production database were not accessed. Staging/deployment acceptance remains necessary.

The reported native/USD arithmetic and authorization obstruction were reproduced against unchanged .31 source. The actual live entered values, evidence, policy approval and database contents were not supplied. The release does not assert how a live rate of 1 was entered or automatically infer a production repair rate.

Local statements generated/downloaded promptly on both baseline and new code. The deployment-specific statement stall was not reproduced; private Chrome profiles, bounded execution/resource use, clear failures and actual download tests address confirmed reliability risks. Local timings are included without claiming a measured production speedup.

Only the confirmed reference/cache/routing consumers are changed. Existing deep-routing and stale-read regressions are rerun; this is not a speculative rewrite of every legacy page. Historical voided cost rows from older releases remain audited history; new corrections reuse the current operational identity and original deep links resolve through correction history.

Advance credit retains the established KRW carrying-balance policy. Original payment currencies and native amounts remain separate. A new commercial policy for denomination/revaluation of supplier advances is not silently introduced. Policy-dependent FX/fee and reversal proposals remain pending until their required approvals/posting checks pass.

The read-only valuation audit targets the durable FX snapshot engine. Older manual/legacy payments without such snapshots require individual source/evidence review and a separately approved repair case. No production-wide bulk repair is performed.

Downgrading after stable-projection revisions requires the compatibility review described in DEPLOYMENT_AND_ROLLBACK.md. Do not overwrite newer financial data with an old backup.
