# Blue Ocean Market V26.5.0

- Business-unit dashboard refresh and strict unit scoping fixed.
- Business-unit switching opens the selected unit dashboard automatically.
- Foldable left navigation with icons and responsive behavior.
- Notifications scoped to the active business unit with direct navigation.
- Excavator Machines / Deals cards no longer show payment status; sold machines show realized profit/loss only.
- Excavator payments and all machine/deal cost types synchronize to Finance with source links and historical backfill.
- Payment/cost deletion or cancellation requires a reason and voids the linked Finance entry while preserving audit history.
- Pakistani-buyer resale remains manual and PKR-based with no automatic resale-profit calculation.
