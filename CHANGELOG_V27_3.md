# Blue Ocean Market V27.3.0

## Meetings redesign
- Reused the existing sidebar Meetings tab; no duplicate Meetings navigation item was added.
- Expanded Meetings into a role-aware and business-unit-aware workspace.
- Users can create meetings within their authorized unit; CEO can arrange cross-unit meetings from consolidated context.
- CEO can invite users across units and arrange executive/all-unit-head, Finance, management and selected-user meetings.
- Added meeting type, primary unit/scope, date/time, duration, priority, recurrence, location, online link, agenda, summary, attachments, minutes, decisions and lifecycle status.
- Added attendee invitation records and Accepted/Tentative/Declined responses.
- Added meeting detail view, minutes/decision review and open action tracking.
- Meeting action items can create linked Tasks so follow-up contributes to workload/performance tracking.
- Managers are restricted to their authorized unit; regular users primarily see meetings they organize or attend; CEO can use unit or consolidated scope.
- Meeting invitations/actions generate notifications and records preserve audit history.
